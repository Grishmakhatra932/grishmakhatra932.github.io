FLAGSHIP PROJECT

EDIFlow Canada

ANSI X12 EDI Integration & Observability Platform

The problem

Build a realistic North American B2B integration workflow capable of receiving ANSI X12 purchase orders, validating business data, preventing duplicate processing and managing downstream ERP failures.

What I built

Developed a FastAPI integration service for ANSI X12 850 Purchase Orders with ISA, BEG, N1 and PO1 parsing, business validation, duplicate detection and ERP-compatible data mapping.

Reliability & auditing

Implemented API-key authentication, retry and permanent-failure simulation, PostgreSQL persistence and audit tracking for completed, rejected and failed EDI transactions.

Monitoring

Containerized the platform with Docker Compose and exposed application metrics through Prometheus, with Grafana dashboards monitoring transaction outcomes and platform health.
